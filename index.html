From watching to making

A place for the whole process.

Follow the streams you have permission to use, find useful moments, and turn them into clips.

01 / MONITOR

Build your watchlist.

Group creators by platform and see available live status, viewer counts, and previews. Availability depends on each platform and your configured data providers.

02 / FIND

Surface the moments.

Local audio, video, and transcript analysis help identify candidate highlights. Configured chat and viewer signals can add context to the selection.

03 / CREATE

Make the clip yours.

Render clips with saved editor presets, including vertical formats. Review the result, keep the local file, and use supported sharing options when configured.

Account connections

Choose what you connect.

Account authorization happens with the platform. You review the requested permissions there, then return to the ClipCraze app running locally.

TikTok Login Kit is currently being tested with authorized sandbox accounts. Public availability and extra capabilities depend on platform access and approval.

01

Basic TikTok account identity

The default login requests user.info.basic to identify the connected account and display its account name. It does not grant video posting permission.

02

Additional access is optional

Video metrics or inbox upload require separate permissions and supported API access. An inbox upload still requires you to finish the post in TikTok.

03

Disconnect when you choose

Disconnect locally in Developer Settings to remove the saved account tokens. You can also revoke access in your platform account settings.

Your workspace

Made to run locally.

The dashboard, media processing, and saved clips run on your computer. This public website explains the project; it does not host the dashboard or connect to your local files.
