Visiting this information site does not connect your social accounts or access the ClipCraze dashboard, media, or credentials on your computer. The app currently runs locally and is in development.

1. Who and what this covers

ClipCraze is maintained by the ClipCraze project maintainer, reachable at ramonlvazq@gmail.com ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy covers this public information website and the local ClipCraze development app.

These are separate environments. This website explains the project. A copy of the app runs on its user's computer and makes requests to the platforms and services configured by that user.

2. Information handled by this website

This is a static information website. We have not added account registration, analytics, advertising trackers, submission forms, or application cookies to it.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes and may process technical request information, such as browser or device information, the requested URL, and request time, to provide and protect its services. Its infrastructure may process this information outside your country. GitHub controls its infrastructure logging and retention; see the GitHub Privacy Statement for more information.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message, and any information you choose to include. We use that information to respond, provide support, address privacy requests, and maintain relevant correspondence. Please do not send passwords, client secrets, access tokens, or private media.

TikTok account access

The default TikTok login requests user.info.basic for basic account identity, such as the account's identifier and display name. OAuth tokens are used to make the requests you authorize. ClipCraze does not receive your TikTok password through Login Kit.

Optional video metrics or inbox upload require additional permissions and platform access. The current integration is being tested in TikTok sandbox with authorized target accounts. Basic login does not provide global TikTok LIVE discovery, chat access, or posting permission.

Optional analysis

Audio, video, and transcript features process media to identify candidate highlights. If installed and enabled, optional face or emotion analysis may process faces appearing in frames. Obtain any required consent before processing other people's information. These outputs can be inaccurate.

4. When information goes to external services

Running locally does not mean that every feature works offline. The app sends requests to the platforms and providers you configure. Those services receive the request data needed for the enabled function, and may receive technical information such as your IP address.

  • Login and account APIs: the selected platform handles authorization and receives the app credentials, authorization code, tokens, or API parameters required by its protocol.
  • Stream, metadata, and chat providers: selected handles, source URLs, and provider credentials may be sent to retrieve media, stream status, or chat.
  • Embeds and external links: opening a platform embed or website may allow that platform to process request information and use its own cookies or similar technologies.
  • Sharing and publishing: enabling a supported upload or publishing action sends the clip and relevant captions, titles, or settings to the destination platform or configured provider. TikTok inbox uploads require the user to finish posting in TikTok.
  • Other optional services: a separately configured external processing service receives the content and parameters needed for its selected feature.

These services handle information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information or use local app media for advertising. This static website does not automatically receive your local workspace or account tokens. If you voluntarily send support information, it is handled as support correspondence.

5. Storage, security, and retention

The app stores its workspace on the computer where it runs. In the current Windows version, saved OAuth tokens and developer setup credentials use Windows Data Protection API protected storage. This protection does not cover every local file: clips, transcripts, settings, logs, models, and other media or records can be ordinary files on disk.

Temporary captures may be removed by app cleanup. Saved clips, records, and configuration may remain until you remove them or change the relevant retention settings. The project does not promise a single deletion period for all local data. Copies and backups you or your storage provider create have their own retention.

Keep your computer, user account, backups, and project folders secure. If your workspace is in a synchronized folder, your chosen synchronization provider may receive those files. We do not control its processing or retention.

We retain support correspondence only as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant records, and meet any applicable obligations. Hosting providers and connected services apply their own retention practices. We cannot delete copies held solely on your computer or independently by a platform.

6. Your choices and deletion controls

  1. Stop processing: stop monitoring or close the local app to stop its processing. Adjust watchlists and disable optional integrations or publishing settings you do not want.
  2. Remove saved account tokens: use Disconnect locally in Developer Settings. This removes the saved OAuth connection for that platform; it does not delete existing media or developer app credentials.
  3. Revoke platform access: remove ClipCraze's authorization in the connected platform's account or security settings. Provider-held information and uploaded posts must be managed with that provider.
  4. Remove local information: after stopping the app, remove the clips, transcripts, logs, models, credentials, or other workspace files you no longer want. Check any backup or synchronization copies separately. Deleting credentials or configuration may require setup again.
  5. Request help or deletion of information we hold: email ramonlvazq@gmail.com. Describe your request and identify the relevant correspondence without sending credentials or unnecessary sensitive information.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or to object to certain processing. Contact us about information we hold. We may need to verify a request and will respond as required by applicable law. For data held only by a platform or hosting provider, use that provider's privacy controls as well.

7. Children

ClipCraze is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information through this website. Connected platforms may have higher minimum ages or other eligibility requirements. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can investigate and address it.
